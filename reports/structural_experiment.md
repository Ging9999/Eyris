- **Drawdown brake.** It does what it says: the worst train drawdown falls from 5.8% to 2.9–5.3%. But selling after a fall locks in
  the loss and misses the rebound, so the return and Sharpe ranks drop more than the drawdown rank rises. The brake is computed only
  from prices visible at each deadline.
- **ERC** reweights more as correlations move, which doubles turnover. Its Sharpe is also worse than plain inverse vol on these 30 names.

## Gross exposure from 2% to 70%

Band and min trade were scaled with gross, so small books can still be entered.

Sharpe does not depend on gross, and a tiny book nearly zeroes drawdown and turnover. Even so, the lost return rank costs more.
**0.5 is the best level in every period**, on both the selection score and mean rank. This is the strongest evidence so far
for keeping gross at 0.5 for Official.
