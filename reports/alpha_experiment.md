# Alpha tilt experiment

LightGBM regressor on 8 cross-sectionally ranked price/volume features (1-bar and 1-day reversal, 5/20/40-day momentum, 20-day volatility, volume surprise, 1-day range). Fitted on 2021-2024 rounds (labels purged at the boundary) and scored out of sample on 2025-01..09. The tilt is `w_i * exp(tilt * z_i)` on the selected inverse-vol weights, with the same gross and cap. Generated by `python scripts/alpha_experiment.py`.

Baseline without alpha: median 6.50, mean 6.42.

## Recommendation: **drop**

- The signal is weak: rank IC is about 0.02 with t ≈ 1.7 for the 1-day horizon, and about 0.01 for 1 week.
- Tilts large enough to move returns (≥ 0.5) add roughly +0.1 to +0.2 pp of 15-day return. They also multiply turnover by 3–9×, which costs far more rank on the turnover metric than they gain on return and Sharpe.
- The small tilt (0.1) leaves the median rank unchanged (6.50). Its 0.07 better mean rank is within noise and fails the keep rule (it must beat the baseline on median *and* mean).
- The code stays in place behind `use_alpha` / `tilt` (off in `models/params.json`). The model files are not committed.
