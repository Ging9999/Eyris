# Research-backed upgrades: experiment

Generated by `python scripts/research_experiment.py`. Each idea got a small pre-declared grid on top of the
live configuration and was scored with the tuning rule: the mean of the median 15-day rank percentiles in the full and
active reference fields (0 = always first), tie-broken on the worst window. Adopting an idea requires beating the
baseline on 2025 validation **and** not losing on 2021-24 train windows.

## Ideas and sources

| Idea | Source | What was tested |
|---|---|---|
| Volatility-managed exposure | Moreira & Muir (2017); [Man Group, *The Impact of Volatility Targeting*](https://www.man.com/insights/the-impact-of-volatility-targeting); [AQR, *Chasing Your Own Tail (Risk)*](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/White-Papers/AQR-Chasing-Your-Own-Tail-Risk-Revisited.pdf?sc_lang=en) | exposure = min(max gross, target vol / forecast vol), targets 6-15%, max gross 0.7/1.0 |
| Trend overlay | Time-series momentum (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen); AQR tail-risk work | cut exposure to 0 or 50% when the equal-weight basket's 5-40 day return is negative |
| Hierarchical Risk Parity | [López de Prado (2016)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/305747867_Building_Diversified_Portfolios_that_Outperform_Out_of_Sample) | HRP book instead of inverse-vol, 20/40-day covariance |
| EWMA risk | Man AHL-style EWMA vol ([overview](https://navnoorbawa.substack.com/p/inside-the-168b-systematic-fund-making-money-while-others-lose)) | half-life 5/10/20 days |
| Overnight vs intraday | [Lou, Polk & Skouras, JFE 2019](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v134y2019i1p192-213.html) | checked in the data first (below) |
| Cost-aware trade filter | [ML strategies under costs](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00060) | already in the agent (no-trade band + min trade) |

**Overnight effect:** for these 30 stocks in 2021-2025 the equal-weight basket earned +2.4 bp/day overnight against +3.75 bp/day
intraday, so the effect does not hold here. Holding risk only overnight would also cost about 20 bp/day in fees. Not built.

## Verdict: keep the current model

- **No idea beat the baseline** on the competition objective in validation. Only EWMA (half-life 20) tied it.
  EWMA lost slightly on train and gained slightly on the holdout, so the result is noise either way. Not adopted.
- **The trend overlay is the one real trade-off.** It roughly halves the worst 15-day drawdown in validation (7.9% to 4.0%;
  train 5.8% to 5.1%) at the cost of median return. That costs it a little rank, so it is rejected under the rule. It would
  be the first switch to flip if you would rather protect against a crash during the Official window than
  maximize expected rank (`gross_mode: "trend", trend_days: 20, trend_floor: 0.5`).
- **Volatility targeting and HRP** cost rank in every period. HRP concentrates into a few low-vol clusters, which helps
  drawdown but hurts return. Vol targeting mostly re-learns the fixed 50% exposure the tuner already picked.
- **Why the gains are small:** the agent is already a low-vol, 50%-cash, near-buy-and-hold book. The research ideas
  mainly work the same exposure dial that tuning already set, and anything that trades more pays for it in the turnover rank.

All options remain available in `Params` (`halflife_days`, `gross_mode`, `vol_target`, `trend_days`, `trend_floor`,
`risk_method="hrp"`), and they are covered by tests.
