# Market sentiment: contrarian VIX overlay

Generated by `python scripts/sentiment_experiment.py` (8 pre-registered variants) and `python scripts/sentiment_sensitivity.py`
(neighbourhood and paired tests), 2026-10-02. Raw results are in the matching `.json` files.

## Why VIX and not news sentiment

Per-stock news sentiment (LLM or FinBERT scoring headlines) cannot be validated honestly. There is no timestamped
historical headline archive in reach (Yahoo RSS returns only the latest ~20 per stock). The approved LLMs have also read
the 2021-25 news (Look-Ahead-Bench). The reduce-only news veto (`eyris/news.py`) is the safe form of that idea.

Market-wide sentiment from the CBOE VIX ("fear index") is public, free on Yahoo (`^VIX`, `^VIX3M`), and has full
daily history. For a round on day d, only closes of days **strictly before d** are used, so there is no lookahead.

## Pre-registered variants

Signals:
- VIX / VIX3M > 1 (backwardation);
- VIX > 25;
- VIX z-score vs the last 60 days > 2 (a spike);
- continuous median(VIX) / VIX.

Each was applied in two directions:
- de-risk: gross × 0.5 on fear;
- contrarian: gross × 1.4 on fear, i.e. 0.5 → 0.7.

Selection score: lower is better. Mean rank is the average overall rank in the full field, also lower is better.

De-risking on fear loses everywhere, which is consistent with the rejected vol-target, trend and drawdown-brake overlays.
Adding on fear is the only direction with a positive sign.

## Robustness

- **Neighbourhood:**
  - Validation: boosts of 1.4-1.6 improve mean rank at every threshold (VIX 20-30, z 1.5-3).
  - Train 2021-24: flat. VIX ≥ 25 slightly helps and VIX 20-22.5 hurts. Selection is unchanged or slightly worse.
  - Holdout: improves wherever the overlay triggered.
- **Paired per-window test** (non-overlapping 15-day windows, overlay vs current): no difference is significant.
  The best is t = -1.7 on the holdout (n = 4). Against the active field on train, VIX > 25 made 14 windows worse and 4 better.
- **Tail risk:** in the 2022 bear market VIX stayed above 25 for weeks, so the overlay added exposure into the decline.
  Worst train drawdown rose from 5.8% to 7.9%.

## Verdict: built, off by default

VIX > 25 / ×1.4 and z > 2 / ×1.4 pass the formal adoption rule (better on validation, tie on train) and improve the holdout.
The evidence is still at noise level, and the overlay is really a conditional gross decision (0.7 when fearful). Gross is
decided after Live Validation, so this is implemented, tested and **off** (`vix_mode: "off"` in `models/params.json`).

To enable it: set `"vix_mode": "level", "vix_threshold": 25, "vix_boost": 1.4`. The live pre-step then fetches `^VIX`/`^VIX3M`,
logs `vix_prev_close`, `vix_z` and `vix_multiplier` in `decision_log.json`, and falls back to multiplier 1 on any error.
At VIX ≈ 16 (2026-10-02) it would be inactive unless a selloff happens during the competition.
