# Overnight search: low-vol tilt strength, concentration, caps, wide bands

Generated by `python scripts/overnight_research.py` (2026-10-02, about 31 min). Raw results are in `overnight_validation.csv`,
`overnight_shortlist.csv` and `overnight_research.json`.

**Verdict: no change. The current configuration stays.** None of the 8,370 configurations passed the adoption rule
(beat current on 2025-01..09 validation AND not lose on 2021-24 train).

## What was searched

The new knobs are in `eyris/risk.py` and `eyris/config.py`. Their defaults reproduce the current agent exactly.

| Knob | Values | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| `vol_power` | 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3 | weights ∝ vol^-p; 2 = inverse variance (stronger low-vol tilt) |
| `top_k` | all, 10, 15, 20 | hold only the k lowest-vol names |
| `stock_cap` | 0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.15 | per-name cap |
| `band` | 0.05, 0.10, 0.20 | wider no-trade band (closer to buy-and-hold) |
| `gross` | 0.3 – 0.85 | reported only; 0.5 vs 0.7 is a decision after Live Validation |
| `lookback_days`, `lam` | 20/30/40, 0.25/0.5 | crossed with the above |

Scoring used `agentic_research.select_score`: the mean median-rank percentile vs the full, active and LLM-style fields.
The top 40 validation configs (overall, and at gross 0.5) were then checked on train (stride 5) and the holdout.

## Results

| Config | Validation | Train 2021-24 | Holdout Q4 (indicative) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Current** (invvol p=1, all 30, cap 0.10, gross 0.5, band 0.05) | 0.194 | 0.199 | 0.176 |
| Best validation: p=1.5, cap 0.06, gross 0.85, band 0.20 | **0.190** | 0.216 | 0.196 |
| p=2, cap 0.06, gross 0.70, band 0.20 | 0.191 | 0.213 | 0.203 |
| Cap 0.06, otherwise current | 0.194 (tie) | **0.197** | 0.176 |

Lower is better (0 = always first).

- **The only validation winners were high-gross variants, and they lost on train and holdout.** The 0.004 validation edge is
  within the noise of about 12 independent 15-day windows in 2025-01..09. This is also evidence for keeping **gross 0.5** for Official.
- **At gross 0.5, every knob ties or loses on validation.** The metric is a median of rank scores, so small weight changes
  rarely move a window's rank. A 6% cap is marginally better on train but only ties on validation, so the rule does not adopt it.
- **`top_k` hurts.** With 10 names the best mean LLM-field rank is 3.49, against 3.22 for the current config. Diversification across all 30 is part of the
  drawdown edge.
- **Wider bands change almost nothing.** After the initial allocation the agent already trades rarely (median turnover 0.0048 per round,
  which is the entry itself). The only team that beats it on turnover is a team holding cash.

## Conclusion

The current configuration is at a flat, robust optimum for this design. Further parameter searching on the same data
would mostly fit noise. The remaining levers are outside the backtest: the actual competitor field (seen in Validation),
and live reliability (correct holdings, no stale state, no missed or invalid rounds).
